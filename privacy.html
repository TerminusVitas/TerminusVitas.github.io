Grim Tally – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Grim Tally does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Data on your device. Player names, deck names, backgrounds and photos you choose, saved profiles, settings, and game history are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the app's data deletes them.

Photos. If you pick a photo as a player background, it is shrunk and saved on your device only. The app does not upload it.

Third-party services. The app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your device's IP address as part of a normal web request (see Google's privacy policy). Links to Scryfall open that website in your browser, where Scryfall's privacy policy applies.

Children. The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
