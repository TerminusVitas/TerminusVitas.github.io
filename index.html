Settings

Life

Tap the left or right side of a player's area for −1 or +1. Hold for −10 or +10, and keep holding to keep going. The small number above the total shows the recent change.

Poison

Tap the drop in the bottom bar to add 1. Hold it to remove 1.

Commander damage & counters

Tap the swords to open that player's counters menu. Add commander damage from each opponent there (it also lowers life). After that, a colored bar per attacker appears above the bottom bar: tap it for +1, hold for −1. The same menu has poison, more counters (energy, experience, rad, speed, commander tax), monarch, initiative, partner commanders, "can't lose at 0 life", life history and Concede.

Look & profiles

Tap the palette to pick a background color or photo, add a deck name, or save the player as a profile to reuse next game. Tap a player's banner to rename them.

Turns & undo

The hourglass next to the menu button passes the turn: the active player glows gold and gets a turn timer. Use "Random first" below to pick who starts. The arrow undoes the last change. Either button can be hidden in Turns.

Death & victory

A player is out at 0 life, 10 poison, or 21 damage from one commander. Tap a fallen player to revive them. When one player (or team) is left, they get the victory screen and the game is saved to History & stats.

This menu

Look up cards on Scryfall, change the player count, starting life, mode (Two-Headed Giant), day/night, sound, themes and layout, roll dice, and reset the game (top, tap twice).

Tip

Your game, names and settings are saved on this phone automatically.
