APP NAME (max 30 characters)
Grim Tally: Life Counter

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 characters)
A pixel-art life counter for Commander, with 10 animated themes.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4,000 characters)
Grim Tally is a life counter built for real card game tables: easy to read from across the table, quick to tap, and full of pixel-art charm.

TRACK EVERYTHING
• 1–8 players, with each seat rotated to face its player
• Tap for ±1, hold for ±10
• Commander damage from every opponent, including partner commanders
• Poison, energy, experience, rad, speed and commander tax
• Monarch, initiative, and day/night
• Two-Headed Giant mode with shared team life

RUN THE GAME
• Turn tracker with a turn timer and round count
• Undo for any slip of the finger
• Random first player, d20, coin flip and planar die
• Card lookup on Scryfall
• Life history for every player

10 ANIMATED THEMES
Fantasy, Space, Ocean, Haunted, Volcano, Forest, Arcane, Frost, Desert and Oil & Porcelain. Each theme has its own colors, banners, background animation and death effect: twinkling stars, rising bubbles, drifting fog, glowing embers and more.

EPIC DEATHS & VICTORIES
When a player falls, the Reaper arrives with a death message. Revive them in one tap, or let the last one standing claim the crown.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Pick backgrounds by mana color, any color, or your own photo
• Add deck names and save player profiles for next game
• Game history and win stats for your playgroup
• Plain-numbers option for easy reading

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything is saved on your device, and the app works without internet.
